# ADR 0005 - Customer Portal Deferred

## Status

Superseded by ADR 0008.

## Decision

Customer portal/login is deferred until the internal workflows are stable.

## Context

RainTech needs a reliable internal delivery platform first. Customer access introduces authentication, authorization, tenant boundaries, audit requirements, and support expectations.

## Consequences

- V1 may include a public/open intake experience.
- V2-V5 remain internal-first.
- V7 becomes the customer portal milestone after hosted security foundations are planned.
